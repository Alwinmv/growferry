Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

At Growferry, accessible at https://www.growferry.in, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or communicate with us.

By using our website or submitting your details through it, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, mobile number, and other contact details you voluntarily provide.
  • Enquiry Information: Details about your business, project requirements, service interests, and the messages you submit through our contact form.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and general request data that our hosting provider may record automatically when you visit the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Understand your business and project requirements.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, and project-related communications.
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

Growferry does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers, where necessary to operate our website or deliver our services. These include our website hosting provider (Vercel), our email delivery provider (Resend), which sends your contact form message to our inbox, our email service (Gmail), and Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces used on this website.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or by a lawful request from an authorised authority.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. Our hosting and font providers may process basic technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the website.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. If we introduce non-essential cookies or tracking technologies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, seek your consent first.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, provide our services, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise questions or complaints about how your information is handled.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
